*   A Digital Transformation (DT) Maturity Assessment Framework was developed for large, government-owned corporations (GOCs), primarily Australian Government Organizations.
*   The framework incorporates Building Information Modelling (BIM), digital engineering (DE), and critical information management.
*   It assesses DT maturity across 56 indicators, utilizing the Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) for ranking based on expert opinion.
*   The assessment tool facilitates data input and evaluation to determine DT status and identify areas for improvement.
*   The research involved a multi-university collaborative project originating from Australia.
*   The framework is relevant to asset management, asset delivery, and asset performance.
