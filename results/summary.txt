*   A maturity assessment framework has been created to evaluate digital transformation (DT) within large infrastructure asset owners and government-owned corporations (GOCs).
*   The framework incorporates key elements such as digital transformation, asset lifecycle management, Building Information Modelling (BIM), Digital Engineering (DE), and data science.
*   It utilizes the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) and expert input to assess DT maturity through a seven-category, 56-indicator tool.
*   The assessment aims to identify current DT status, establish improvement targets, and align strategies with organizational objectives.
*   Technologies like BIM, DE, IoT, and AI are leveraged to enhance planning, design, operation, and maintenance of assets.
*   Data integration and critical information management competencies are integral components of the framework.
