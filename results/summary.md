# AI Study Assistant - Summary

*   A maturity assessment framework was developed for digital transformation within large infrastructure asset owners and government-owned corporations.
*   The framework employs the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) alongside Building Information Modelling (BIM) and related technologies like Digital Engineering (DE), data science, IoT, and AI.
*   It assesses digital transformation across the asset lifecycle, focusing on management, delivery, and performance improvements.
*   An Excel-based tool incorporating 56 indicators, driven by expert input via AHP, facilitates maturity evaluation and target setting.
*   Strategic alignment with organizational objectives is critical for maximizing the impact of digital transformation on efficiency, innovation, and safety. 
*   Key elements integrated within the framework include BIM, data integration, value of information, and critical information management competencies.
