# AI Study Assistant - Summary

*   A maturity assessment framework was developed to evaluate digital transformation (DT) within large infrastructure asset owners and government-owned corporations (GOCs).
*   The framework employs the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) alongside Building Information Modelling (BIM) and other technologies like data science, IoT, and AI. 
*   It assesses DT maturity through a seven-category, 56-indicator tool focused on digital engineering practices, value of information, data integration, and critical information management competencies. 
*   The framework supports organizations in evaluating their current DT status and identifying areas for improvement across asset lifecycle management.
*   Digital transformation is recognized as vital for enhancing efficiency, innovation, and safety within these sectors.
