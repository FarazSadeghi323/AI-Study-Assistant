# AI Study Assistant - Summary

*   A maturity assessment framework for digital transformation (DT) was developed targeting large infrastructure asset owners and government-owned corporations (GOCs).
*   The framework integrates Building Information Modelling (BIM), digital engineering, data integration, value of information, and critical information management.
*   It employs the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) along with expert input to create a seven-category, 56-indicator tool for DT maturity evaluation.
*   This tool supports setting improvement targets aligned with strategic organizational objectives related to efficiency, innovation, and safety.
*   The research involves collaboration across multiple universities and institutions.
*   Digital processes including data science, IoT, and AI are key components of this transformation within the building and infrastructure sectors.
