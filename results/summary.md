# AI Study Assistant - Summary

*   A maturity assessment framework was developed to evaluate digital transformation (DT) within large infrastructure asset owners and government-owned corporations (GOCs).
*   The framework integrates Building Information Modelling (BIM), Digital Engineering (DE), data science, IoT, AI, and critical information management competencies.
*   It focuses on assessing DT maturity across asset management, delivery, and performance.
*   The assessment utilizes the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) with a seven-category, 56-indicator tool.
*   Strategic alignment with organizational objectives is key to maximizing efficiency, innovation, and safety through DT initiatives.
*   Data integration and value of information are central components of the framework.
*   Research was conducted by researchers from multiple Australian universities.
