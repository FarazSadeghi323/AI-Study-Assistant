# AI Study Assistant - Summary

*   A maturity assessment framework was developed to evaluate digital transformation within large infrastructure asset owners and government-owned corporations.
*   The framework incorporates Building Information Modelling (BIM), Digital Engineering (DE), data science, IoT, and AI technologies.
*   It assesses DT maturity across the entire asset lifecycle – including management, delivery, and performance.
*   The Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) was utilized with expert input to rank key indicators for evaluation.
*   A practical assessment tool, utilizing a 56-indicator Excel spreadsheet, facilitates data input and maturity analysis.
*   Strategic alignment with organizational objectives is essential for successful digital transformation initiatives.
*   Digital technologies enable enhanced asset planning, design, construction, operation, and maintenance. 
*   Data integration and critical information management competencies are integral components of the framework.
*   The tool supports understanding current DT status and setting future targets.
